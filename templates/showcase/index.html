Relational Architecture & Entity-Relationship Model
Third Normal Form (3NF)

The database consists of 12 normalized tables connected by strictly enforced foreign keys with appropriate ON DELETE RESTRICT and CASCADE rules. Comma-separated attributes have been eliminated via junction tables (student_skills, job_allowed_departments, job_required_skills) satisfying 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF.

First Normal Form (1NF)
All columns store atomic values. Multi-valued student skills and job requirements are moved into relational junction tables.
Second Normal Form (2NF)
In 1NF and no non-key attribute is partially dependent on any composite candidate key (e.g. student_skills has primary key (student_id, skill_id)).
Third Normal Form (3NF)
In 2NF and no transitive functional dependencies exist. Department names are stored exclusively in departments, not duplicated in students.
